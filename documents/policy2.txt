HR Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays declared by the company.

Sick leave requires a medical certificate if the absence exceeds 2 consecutive working days. Employees must inform their reporting manager before 10 AM on the day of absence.

Maternity leave is granted for 26 weeks as per company policy, and paternity leave is granted for 10 working days.

Unused leave can be carried forward to the next year up to a maximum of 10 days. Any leave beyond this limit will lapse at the end of the calendar year.

Employees must apply for planned leave at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal.
