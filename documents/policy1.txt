IT Support Policy

All employees must reset their passwords every 90 days. Password must contain at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.

If an employee forgets their password, they should contact the IT helpdesk at extension 4455 or email support@company.com.

Laptops issued to employees must be returned within 5 working days of resignation or termination. Employees are responsible for any damage caused due to negligence during the usage period.

VPN access is mandatory for all employees working remotely. VPN credentials are issued by the IT department and must not be shared with anyone outside the organization.
