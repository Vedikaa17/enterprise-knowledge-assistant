Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees can claim reimbursement for business-related travel, accommodation, and meals incurred during official work trips. All claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense being incurred.

Original receipts or invoices are mandatory for any claim above ₹500. Claims without valid receipts may be rejected or require additional manager approval.

Local conveyance for client meetings can be claimed at company-approved rates. Employees using personal vehicles must submit odometer readings along with fuel bills.

Reimbursement requests must be submitted through the finance portal and approved by the reporting manager before being processed by the finance team.

Any expense exceeding ₹10,000 requires prior written approval from the department head before the trip or purchase.
